Custom Book Details

Create additional information fields for your books. These fields will appear in the Add Book form and CSV import mapping.

How custom fields work

  • Shared Book Detail: Information applies to all copies in a book group, such as Publisher, ISBN, or Language.
  • Individual Copy Detail: Information belongs to a specific physical copy, such as Condition, Remarks, or Copy Status.
  • Fields can also be displayed as columns in the Catalog Desk.
